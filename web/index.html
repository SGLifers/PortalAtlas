How these routes are calculated

Walls are nodes. Placement angles and direct movement are directed edges. Ray tests use asset collision hulls and render geometry as a fallback; primitive bounds remain a proxy.

Times and difficulty are estimates. Portal chains assume an active portal at the start wall and replace the opposite portal at each step. Launch arrivals stop the route until their outgoing motion is modeled. Routes around corners are not inferred.

Fastest minimizes estimated seconds. Easiest minimizes accumulated effort; the summary shows the hardest step. Balanced weighs both.
